ESP32 Smart 4WD IoT & AI RC Car

Architects & Developers: Chamidu Madushan & Gemini

📌 Project Overview

This project represents an advanced, high-performance, wireless-controlled 4WD robotic vehicle integrated with IoT capabilities, live video streaming, and intelligent sensor arrays. Designed for remote surveillance, autonomous navigation, and dynamic lighting control, it bridges cutting-edge microcontrollers with robust mechanical assembly.

⚙️ Hardware Architecture & Sub-systems

🧠 Processing & Vision

  • ESP32 3MP Cam Board (Live Video & Wi-Fi Control)
  • Auxiliary ESP32 Microcontroller
  • 2-Axis Camera Pan-Tilt Mount
  • Servomotors for Precise Tracking

⚡ Power & Management

  • Dual 3000mAh High-Capacity Li-ion Batteries
  • DC-DC Buck Converter & Regular IC
  • Voltage Sensor for Real-time Battery Tracking

🏎️ Drive & Motor Control

  • Robust 4WD Chassis & Motor Kit
  • Mini Dual H-Bridge Motor Driver
  • PWM-based Speed & Direction Regulation

🛰️ Sensors & Telemetry

  • Ultrasonic Sensor (Obstacle Detection)
  • Ambient Light Sensor
  • 1.3-inch OLED Display (Live Telemetry)

💡 Lighting & Aesthetics Subsystem

Headlights (High-Intensity Front Illumination) Ground Flow RGB LEDs (Underglow Effects) Arrow Yellow LEDs x4 (Signaling & Indicators)
